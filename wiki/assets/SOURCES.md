# Image sources

The wiki uses two locally stored, annotated/cropped images:

- `combat-annotated.jpg` is an annotated version of screenshot 2 from the
  [Slay the Spire Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/646570/Slay_the_Spire/).
  Direct source image:
  [`ss_01aa3e7759e457bfbf2422f31c325d7b3ba8a6eb`](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/646570/ss_01aa3e7759e457bfbf2422f31c325d7b3ba8a6eb.1920x1080.jpg).
  The annotations identify the hand and enemy intent. The HP labels are
  intentionally invented, plausible teaching values; they are not model
  predictions for that screenshot.

- `bash-card.png` is a crop of Bash from screenshot 5 on the same Steam store
  page. Direct source image:
  [`ss_8af73365121b1b1e2d8004fca2cbffb9b9f6d7a5`](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/646570/ss_8af73365121b1b1e2d8004fca2cbffb9b9f6d7a5.1920x1080.jpg).
