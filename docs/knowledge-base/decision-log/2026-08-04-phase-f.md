# 2026-08-04 — Phase F search distillation (5.7.1 + 5.7.2): falsified on the gate A/B

**Date:** 2026-08-04. **Task:** Phase F (ooc-phase-f-plan.md) — train the
policy head on softmax(labelWon/τ) search-distillation targets and measure a
policy-score gate arm against the Q-gate and the V3 reference. 5.7.3 (the
iterative loop) out of scope.

## Decisions

1. **The policy objective lever is falsified on the current Tier-B labels.**
   Soft-target distillation (λ ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1.0}, τ=1.0) did not beat the
   one-hot objective: every distilled-policy gate arm lost to the Q-gate
   (paired, Q better by ~+3..+27pp, significant at m≤0.05) and to the
   reference (best arm −21.1pp [−30.5,−12.5]). `STS_OOC_STRATEGIC_SCORE`
   stays opt-in; `q` remains the default. 5.7.3 stays unchecked.
2. **The K=6 deep-target datagen (5.7.1b) was not run this wave** — the A/B
   was scheduled on the existing K=3 corrected labels per the plan's cheaper
   fallback (the objective, not the horizon, was the question). K=6 remains
   the open follow-up.
3. **λ=1.0 (pure distillation) is rejected**: behavioral top-1 halves (0.44),
   logits collapse to near-uniform (margin 0.01), dist_top1 worsens — the
   one-hot anchor is required.
4. **The score-source env applies only to the V5/V6 non-token, non-mapEnc
   paths** — the token net has no policy-logit method and the V6 map-encoder
   branch feeds different features; both untouched.

## Rationale (labels per the decision-log contract)

- The tie-dominated `labelWon` (median softmax margin 0.000 at every τ;
  entropy 1.54 bits) caps the ranking signal at the argmax, and the
  distilled policy's label-argmax agreement (dist_top1 0.38) equals
  behavior's (0.38) — there is no ranking headroom to distill
  (**Inference**, from §104 Step-1 diagnostic + training metrics).
- Flat policy logits (margin 18.6 → ~1.2) make the margin gate fire on
  almost every decision, so the arm is an aggressive override that
  consistently underperforms the conservative heuristic
  (**Inference**, from the margin distribution).
- The falsification chain from §100 (encoder → objective) is now complete
  on these labels; the remaining lever per §93 is label horizon/quality
  (**Inference**).

## Alternatives considered

- **Skip the A/B after the flat offline metrics** — rejected: the pre-
  registered protocol requires the gameplay measurement; the negative is
  itself the recordable outcome.
- **A/B λ=0.50 only** — rejected in favour of both λ=0.25/0.50 (within
  noise on dist_top1; q_top1 favoured λ=0.50); 8 runs ≈ 15 min.
- **Reuse of the §100 `_state.bin`** — accepted after the λ=0 parity run
  reproduced the baseline epoch-1 metrics exactly (value_mae 9.0578),
  guarding the §100 killed-trainer corruption trap.
