# Decision cases and interpretation

This packet separates recorded actions, reproduced defects and unproven strategy judgments. It covers two fixed first-12 source-order cohorts containing wins and losses: the original public8 discovery reference and the corrected public8/optional-Recall reference. The latter also has all 128 endpoint locations audited. Selected intervention rescues and the Champ loss are explicitly outcome-informed forensics; they cannot estimate population frequencies. Public alternatives for the targeted REST probe were nominated before continuation outcomes, with the actual baseline action known.

The 19 cases comprise three confirmed bugs, four benign or disconfirmed cases,
eleven inconclusive cases and one suspicious untested case. Thus twelve
case-level policy-quality questions remain unresolved. Cases overlap and were
not sampled to estimate a population frequency.

512-pair confirmation launched once at 05:02 and finished at 05:41 Europe/Berlin
on 2026-10-08: 395 versus 381 wins, 27/13 discordances, +2.734375 pp with paired
95% interval [+0.390625, +5.2734375]. The case table was consolidated before
these outcomes; its local uncertainty is not erased by the population result.
