## Important unresolved explanations

### Combat budget, stopping and tactical search

The corrected 128-source reference loses 27 runs, including 13 Act-3 boss deaths. That locates failure, not its cause: preparation, entry health, potions, search variance and tactics all remain possible. In the first12, the three losses show different plausible chains: optional Sphere exposure; attrition before Automaton; and a Brimstone-containing Awakened One run. A winning Brimstone case and singleton final actions are explicit counterexamples to simple blame rules. No unrecorded winning hand sequence should be inferred.

Initial flat exact10k discovery gave 26/32 versus learned-stop 25/32, with a wide paired interval and approximately 2.28 times CPU. Binary terminal utility had exactly the same 32 outcomes as flat10k; zero observed discordance is not equivalence. Both tests predate the Recall correction and cannot be reused as corrected-baseline qualification. Historical #425's selected boss-entry 40k test was also inconclusive at 4.4 times CPU; #400 did not justify broad contextual search-constant adaptation. These results weaken a generic “just search more” priority while leaving sharper stopping, root coverage, ordering and reuse explanations open.

**Consequence:** there is no completed evidence supporting a new combat modification in the final candidate. A future search study should first identify a reproducible public tactical class with independently superior continuations under the intended controller. Repeating a broad budget/parameter sweep now would not resolve nomination cheaply. [Atlas](CORRECTED_REFERENCE_ATLAS.md), [terminal interpretation](../audit/terminal-discovery-interpretation.md), ledger-06 §§369,387.

### Old-model calibration, counterfactual representation and information scope

V4 Q is a raw historical surrogate (`10*labelWon+labelHp` in the inspected trainer), not a calibrated current-policy probability. The floor head, original win head and historical public P0/F2 models have different targets and teacher histories. A logistic output or native export parity does not prove useful *differences* between hypothetical actions. Public8 removes realized hidden encounter IDs from the controlled fallback calculation, but averages the original model; it does not retrain it for the current controller or validate its counterfactual calibration.

Card/shop hypothetical rows remain approximate feature edits rather than exact simulator successors. Offered upgrades, acquisition relic effects, some removals and potion opportunities can matter without appearing in those edits. The isolated shop deck-size correction is real but leaves these approximations. CardWinRank preserves each root's floor take/skip decision and changes only ranking when taking; even a positive result could not settle the best skip threshold, V4-owned card choices or exact-transition scoring. Its completed smoke proves finite scores, default-off parity, trace neutrality and an actual Iron Wave→Burning Pact change. All20 smoke records were takes; runtime skip coverage remains fixture-only. Historical ledger-01 §24's 512-seed win-head null must remain attached to interpretation.

The approved simulator contract preserves some counters/pools. GEN2 redraws hidden order and RNG but retains latent counters and pool membership; its completed report explicitly does not claim an exact posterior of all player-visible history. Historical P0/F2 also retain latent-counter inputs and privileged-teacher label history. No additional live-profile leak has been demonstrated by this audit, but neither public8 nor those exports establish a complete live-observation sensor audit. Final claims must describe the frozen simulator information contract precisely; this limitation must not be silently generalized into human-observation fairness or repaired midway through qualification.

**Consequence:** model calibration and representation are important next-research explanations, not grounds to swap models after screening. Existing-data, source-grouped error/variance diagnostics can inform the next preregistration. Current GEN2 lacks a demonstrated gain and broad source diversity for a qualified replacement. Its post-primary diagnostic design is not treated as a result here. [Fair fallback](FAIR_FALLBACK_IMPLEMENTATION.md), [model inventory](../learning/README.md), [card objective diagnosis](../audit/card-head-objective-diagnosis.md).

### Path, event, shop and campfire ownership

The learned gate, fallback policy and legal menu jointly determine behavior. Removing one action can alter the V4 margin and owner even when the executed action is unchanged. The full-health filter preserves Dream Catcher, forced-only boundaries, Recall filtering and other special actions; source023's first executed divergence is **DIG**, not SMITH. Entry-only Tea Set/Feather benefits do not require REST, while Dream Catcher does. Limit Break's exhaust change and fixed continuation behavior prevent a deterministic upgrade-dominance claim.

Broad RestOff's negative development weakens universal “the learned campfire policy overrests” claims. It does not isolate the full-health subcase. ShopOff tests gate ownership against native bundle planning, which itself omits potions and retains approximate value features. Path projected affordability, potion purchase opportunity cost and broad event ownership have not received controlled population or repeated-state tests. The corrected atlas establishes exposures and legal histories, not their optimality. Removal's old sign prefers high obtain-weight cards; the narrow alternative tests ordering, not universal card quality. Offering/Shrug/Reaper removal can be suspicious without being a mechanics violation or a proven loss cause; Bonfire's different rare-sacrifice reward is correctly excluded from the sign change.

**Consequence:** do not call an ownership ablation a pure value-model test or attribute every downstream decision to the first changed gate. Candidate-specific final reporting should name the actual intervention and its legal boundaries. Path/event/potion hypotheses remain deferred, with no current case strong enough to demand another broad screen before confirmation. [Atlas](CORRECTED_REFERENCE_ATLAS.md), [shared rescue](SHARED_RESCUE_610081023.md), [removal review](REMOVE_OBTAIN_WEIGHT_REVIEW.md).

### Interactions, selection noise and the limits of a worked rescue

RestShop's completed 91/128 is worse descriptively than either component and its paired intervals do not support improvement. This is evidence against assuming beneficial changes compose, not a clean factorial decomposition of all possible interactions. Source023 provides two concrete but distinct mechanisms: FullHp changes late RESTs to DIGs, obtaining Toxic Egg and Anchor; RemoveLow changes early Reaper removal to Strike removal and broadly changes later deck/shop/upgrade decisions. The FullHp arm's actual trajectory remains equal through earlier owner changes, whereas RemoveLow diverges early. Neither the full win nor the value of the two late relics is isolated by those histories. The shared rescue is not an independent replication and authorizes no combined candidate.

Different fresh-development cohorts and choosing among multiple candidates add selection noise. The predeclared greatest-gain/CPU/protocol rule is an exploratory nomination procedure, not a calibrated test across candidates. Completing each fixed cohort, preserving its own advancement/tie rule and opening only one frozen 512-pair comparison is the appropriate existing safeguard. No post hoc seed trimming, retrospective combination or extension can improve that evidence legitimately.

## Implication for nomination and the next research step

No unresolved explanation here requires changing the current frozen development rules. A qualified final contrast can establish a profile-specific whole-run gain without proving the causal share of every decision. Conversely, a useful bug fix, an attractive worked case or good local model fit cannot override failure to qualify. Preserve corrected Fprod in both arms of the selected candidate's immutable producer, record all attempted screens and conditional probes, and keep the holdout untouched until that identity and analysis are frozen.

If no candidate qualifies, the best supported next research direction is **decision-level calibration and counterfactual fidelity under the corrected current teacher**, beginning with already collected source-grouped GEN2 diagnostics and exact public action effects. Whether its obstacle is finite-K selection noise, weak continuation or representation must be distinguished before a larger collection, training run or online planner. This is a next-study direction, not an authorization or promise of gain. Combat work remains justified when a stronger reproducible tactical discriminator appears; the present selected Champ example did not provide one.

Knowledge impact: this is bounded campaign interpretation. It changes no maintained policy fact, runtime default, model, nomination rule or experiment allocation.
