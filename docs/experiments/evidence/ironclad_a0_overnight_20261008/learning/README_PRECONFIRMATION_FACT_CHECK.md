## Concrete corrections

1. **Lines 128–129, “Old public P0/F2 models.”** “Public” is too broad. Both historical 457-feature exports omit the four hidden encounter-queue fields but retain latent potion/rarity counters and privileged-teacher labels. Suggested replacement: “Historical queue-blind P0/F2 models also failed to establish action-selection gains; they retain latent counters and privileged-teacher provenance and are not qualified fair deployment models.” The primary report's limits explicitly make this distinction. Arithmetic/export parity does not resolve it.

2. **Lines 135–136, measured cost “ruled out” a large corpus.** The completed report establishes 392.094290 source plus 14,550.502842 branch CPU seconds, or 4.15072 observed core-hours for 1,968 continuations. It does not specify a rejected corpus size/resource projection proving every large-corpus alternative infeasible. Suggested replacement: “The measured generation cost—4.15072 core-hours for 1,968 branches—together with the inconclusive labels and protected confirmation reserve supported keeping collection bounded rather than scaling a fresh training corpus.” This is a resource/research decision, not a measured impossibility claim.

3. **Lines 110–112, Champ denominator/teacher.** Counts 50/62 versus 51/62 are correct, but add the independent-source denominator and changed continuation controller. Suggested replacement: “From one outcome-informed public combat root, SHRUG won 50/62 paired conditional futures versus 51/62 for END under fresh exact-10k continuation; the result was LOCAL_CONDITIONAL_INCONCLUSIVE. These are not 62 source decisions, and the original retained learned-stop controller was unavailable.” The sign is SHRUG minus END; discordances are 9 SHRUG-only and 10 END-only. The current language must not be read as rejection of all useful Shrug decisions.

4. **Lines 121–125, GEN2 branch denominator — clarification.** The 1,968 branches include all 246 retained actions across both four-draw splits. The primary evaluates 63 selected-vs-baseline decisions on four reserved draws: 252 correlated state/future pairs, clustered in 32 source runs. Add “The primary uncertainty unit is the source run, not the 1,968 branches.” Source coverage is 32 CARD and 31 BOSS states; one source has only a CARD state. The existing equal-source gain and CI are correct and must remain unchanged.

5. **Lines 190–194, next-experiment interpretation and cost.** The proposed K4/K16 comparison is a reasonable *recommended discriminating experiment*, not an established best intervention or a sufficiently powered population qualification. Suggested explanation: “It varies selection precision at identical fresh states while holding teacher and sampler fixed; neither historical P0/F2 model selects actions. Source heterogeneity and sampler/teacher limits remain.” K4 is nested within K16 (0–3 versus 0–15); both choice sets freeze before shared evaluation 16–31. The primary endpoint is K16-choice minus K4-choice with whole-source uncertainty.

6. **Lines 193–194, projection bounds.** The 8.3-hour typical figure is correct for four retained options: 4,096 branches at observed BOSS cost 7.186285 CPU seconds/branch, plus source work. The conservative six-option projection is 6,144 branches and **12.37351 core-hours before tail allowance**. The proposed **16 game-core-hour cap** is P4 × four hours, not a completion guarantee. Include the six-option estimate and the per-source 1,800-second limit, or keep the cap explicitly labeled a proposed hard stop. No launch or training follows from the recommendation.
