## What the comparison identifies

At at most one deterministically selected BOSS_RELIC state per source, hold the legal retained options, continuation policy, public-future sampler and source trajectory fixed. K4 selects from future indices 0–3; K16 selects from 0–15. Both maximize their own empirical final-win value, favor the actual captured baseline action on ties and then use the same canonical-key tie order. Freeze and hash both complete choice sets before inspecting evaluation indices 16–31.

This is one conceptual intervention: the amount of selection evidence available at the same decision. Nested selection draws and shared independent evaluation draws are appropriate coupling. The K16 policy does not learn from evaluation labels, and the old P0/F2 models do not choose actions here. The frozen source-policy choice can be owned by V4 or native fallback; it is not necessarily a V4 choice.

For each allocated source, compute the mean of the 16 paired evaluation differences between its K16 and K4 choices. A **valid source with no eligible retained state contributes exactly zero**. Average across all 32 allocated sources, and bootstrap whole sources 10,000 times with RNG seed 20261008. A strictly positive lower 95% interval bound means local conditional gain; a strictly negative upper bound means local conditional harm; otherwise the result is inconclusive. An invalid, missing or timed-out allocated source/branch blocks clean inference; it must not be reclassified as a valid no-state source or dropped from the denominator.

The threshold is a local diagnostic rule, separate from the closed campaign's whole-run confirmation criterion. No second final champion is selected here. The finished RemoveLow trial's allocation, artifacts, outputs and verdict remain closed and are not pooled into this experiment.

## Competing explanations and power limits

- A positive result supports the claim that this additional selection budget improves these conditional decisions under this teacher/sampler. It does not establish optimal play, live-game equivalence, online runtime feasibility or whole-run policy improvement.
- An inconclusive result can arise from few changed choices, near ties, a conditional win ceiling, only 32 independent source clusters, or teacher/sampler limitations. It does not prove that K4 is sufficient or that selection noise is harmless.
- A negative result is possible even with more selection samples: finite-sample action rankings need not improve monotonically, and candidate outcomes are stochastic and correlated.
- Report the number of valid no-state sources, K4/K16 disagreements, selection ties, retained options, and evaluation ceilings as descriptive diagnostics. Do not choose subgroups, alter K, filter difficult sources or change the primary analysis after viewing results.
- Sixteen evaluation futures per retained state improve within-state precision but are not 512 independent source decisions. Whole-source resampling preserves the source as the unit of inference.
- This isolates K within the existing retained-action set and conditional simulator. It does not test omitted actions or remove inherited latent counter/pool assumptions. Those remain explicit limits even when the runtime information-boundary checks pass.
- Existing corpora/results are admissible for the already recorded cost projection, implementation/helper verification and hypothesis motivation. They do not become fresh source outcomes or augment this experiment's sample.
