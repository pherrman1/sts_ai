# Coupled choices and accounting qualifications

All effects below are offline, source-backed accounting. No sibling was executed.
They are kept separate from Bound B. Unseen random products are not invented.

## 111101032: Pandora's Box and the earlier transform

At floor17 / OOC54 / trace64, Pandora replaces five Strikes and four Defends
with this ordered batch:

Berserk, Inflame, Dropkick, Barricade, Entrench, Dropkick, Fire Breathing,
Wild Strike, Rage.

Both Dropkicks and seven clutter cards are coupled products of this one relic.
None is an independently skippable card reward. Permanent size change:9 ->9,
net0. Offered alternatives are Astrolabe, Runic Dome and boss-relic skip.
Runic Dome/skip forego this only observed acquisition of the Dropkicks;
Astrolabe transforms3 ->3, with unobserved products, so it supplies no established
engine-preserving cardinality reduction. This is not proof that an unexecuted
Astrolabe outcome could never happen to contain a pair.

Bash is a starter. Pommel Strike is a floor7 reward (OOC22), upgraded at floor11
(OOC34); neither comes from Pandora. There are two Fire Breathings: the floor2
event transformed the floor1 Flame Barrier reward into the first; the second
is a Pandora product.

**Do not silently prune the transform dependency.** There are six historical
non-core reward acquisitions including Flame Barrier. Skipping Flame Barrier
removes the historical Transmorgrifier target. One cannot both keep that exact
transform transaction and delete its input. The clean fixed-transaction
REWARD_SKIP_BOUND therefore protects that ancestor and skips the five later
non-engine rewards:18 ->13. It is a conservative executable-choice accounting
variant, not proof that six skips cannot work after natural policy changes.

Naively deleting the transformed Fire Breathing as a sixth reward descendant
would print12 cards, but requires cancelling/retargeting the transform. That is
not the same fixed-choice bound. A conditional12-card projection is:
Ascender's Bane, Bash, Pommel Strike+, Berserk, Inflame, Dropkick, Barricade,
Entrench, Dropkick, Fire Breathing, Wild Strike, Rage.
This same list is achievable in the separate Bound-B accounting by actually
paying the Cleric75g to remove the transformed Fire Breathing (rather than
claiming it was a free skip). No replay of either version is claimed.

Removing a starter before Pandora changes its transform count and product
batch. The historical getTrulyRandomCard loop draws N products then appends
them in reverse. Under an explicitly held historical random stream, reducing
N from9 to8 would truncate the final generated product (Berserk) and retain the
pair; that is a conditional source-level prefix calculation, not a guarantee
that changed policy/encounters reproduce that RNG state. It is excluded from
the independent-removal bound. The initial Neow remove-one alternative is
therefore a coupled choice, not another free post-Pandora removal.

## 111101326: Sensory Stone and Mark of Pain

At floor36 / OOC112 / trace130, the historical choice spends10HP for three
colorless reward groups. Group1 contains Forethought, Jack of All Trades,
Flash of Steel. Group2 contains Secret Weapon, Sadistic Nature, Flash of Steel.
Group3 contains Panacea, Panic Button, Enlightenment. Historical picks occur
in reverse group order: Panic Button OOC113, Flash OOC114, Flash OOC115.

The source generates groups sequentially, so choosing two groups (5HP) retains
the first two offers at that event's fixed RNG state. Picking the two Flashes
would avoid the third group's Panic Button and5HP loss. Single coupled choice
accounting:31 ->30, net-1, preserving the pair. Do not combine this with Bound B
or count Panic Button twice: it is already a skip in Bound A. Choosing only
one group does not preserve the observed two-card pair.

Mark of Pain came from the first boss relic at floor17 / OOC51. Its alternatives
were Cursed Key, Tiny House and skip. It adds **zero permanent deck cards**, but
+1 energy/turn and two temporary Wounds at every battle start. The target has
31 permanent cards and starts with33 active cards, including exactly2 Wounds.
The retained combat records keep2 Wounds; Anger copies increase from3 to5 across
all zones. Those copies are temporary combat inflation, not OOC acquisitions.
Dramatic Entrance/exhaust, Ascender's Bane/exhaust, powers and other combat
effects can reduce the active count separately. A transient all-zone count32
also occurs at a card-selection boundary. None alters the31-card permanent deck.

Replacing Mark of Pain can remove2 temporary Wounds but also loses its energy;
that is not a2-card permanent removal. Tiny House can offer a card, which may
be skipped. Cursed Key can add later chest curses; those are conditional, not
free compression. At floor34 the alternatives to Ectoplasm were Astrolabe,
Sacred Bark and skip; Astrolabe transforms3 ->3, not a size reduction.

We Meet Again at floor19 offered a Headbutt donation,121g donation, or potion
donation for a relic. Historical choice donated Skill Potion and got Vajra.
Both Headbutts are already reward-skipped in Bound A, so the offered Headbutt
cannot be removed again to shrink Bound B. No extra event removal is counted.

## General bound interpretation

These bounds freeze historical offers, path, non-card cash gains and acquired
engine products. They are optimistic structural projections, not simulated
trajectories. They do not promise survival, identical future offers/RNG, retained
engine acquisition under naturally changed policy, or an active pool equal to
permanent deck size. No upgrade is reallocated when its historical target is
skipped. Actual subsequent policy choices are intentionally unmeasured.

Bound B counts mutually exclusive event removal instead of healing, one removal
per shop, rising shop prices75/100/125 (event removals do not increment the shop
counter), explicit foregone purchases and an auditable running cash delta.
It includes only cards still present under Bound A. Non-engine shop cards
foregone as part of funding legal removals are listed separately
from reward skips and actual removed cards. Retained legal sibling keys and
resource plans make these assumptions inspectable.
